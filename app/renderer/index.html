Stemlab

Before the first song, Stemlab downloads its audio engine and the model that pulls songs apart into parts. This happens once.

  • About 1 GB download, a few minutes on a good connection
  • Everything runs on this computer; your songs never leave it

Pull a song apart

        
Add a song and Stemlab splits it into vocals, guitar, piano, bass, drums and everything else, so you can hear each part by itself, change the key, and steady the beat.

        
      

    


    
    

      

        

        

        

        

        
Splitting takes a few minutes per song. You can add more songs while you wait.
